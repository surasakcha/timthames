📲 Put this on the Home Screen

On iPad and iPhone, adding this to the Home Screen keeps your stars safe. Safari clears saved progress after about a week if you only open it as a normal web page.

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add, then open it from the new icon from now on.
